INSTALL THE BLOOMSTALL APP ON YOUR iPHONE

1. Press the THREE DOTS at the bottom right of your screen. (In Safari)[cite: 1]

2. Press SHARE.

3. Swipe UP to show the full SHARE screen.

4. Press ADD TO HOME SCREEN.

The Bloomstall app icon will be added to the last page of your home screen. (We suggest moving it to the first!) ~ ;)
